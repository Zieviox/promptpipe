PP wireframe

Tap through it. Find (⌕) searches chats and every function and lights up the control it points to. Tap ⋯ under a reply to open its actions. Desktop: Ctrl+K or F3 opens Find, Esc goes back.

Design note: a profile could recommend a site (for example Feedback prefers DeepSeek). That would show as a hint when you start a new chat with that profile and never force a switch. Not built in this wireframe.
