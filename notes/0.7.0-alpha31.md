PromptPipe 0.7.0 alpha 31 is the first public alpha of the redesigned app. Install `promptpipe.apk` below (Android 10 or newer). It installs over earlier builds signed with the same key.

## New in 0.7.0

- **A new look**: the whole app has been redrawn. Find (⌕) searches your chats and every setting and lights up where each one lives. The chat list is compact, with pins, folders, drag and drop and multi-select.
- **Quick start**: four short guides of three steps each (welcome and log in, chatting, the chat list, plugins). Each opens the first time you reach its place, and you can replay them from Help.
- **Starter profiles**: Plain, Writing feedback, Dialogue & Role play, and Story & Prose. You pick one before your first message.
- **Each chat has its own profile and switches**: switch the profile or turn plugins on and off from Quick edit (the stack next to the message box), and only that chat changes. Tap the profile name to rename it.
- **Sites per chat**, and the site's page is shown inside the app, with one tab per page, an address bar and reload.
- **Plugins screen**: your profile's rows and a plugin library. Get plugins from the official list or from any plugin repo link a friend sends you. Plugins update on their own, and you can roll an update back.
- **Memory** summarises your side and the AI's side separately.
- **Long messages** of yours fold to a few lines, with Show more.

## Good to know

PromptPipe has no AI of its own. It uses the chat sites you log in to, on your phone, like you would. Use an account you can afford to lose: sites may limit automated use.
